Subject: Request for written Payments policy guidance for Fitly physical clothing exchange membership

Fitly is preparing an invite-only British Columbia pilot and requests written policy guidance before enabling any purchase or external-purchase call to action in its Android app.

The proposed first-release contract is:

- one annual marketplace membership, proposed at CAD 59 plus applicable tax;
- one server-issued grant of 60 Fitly Credits for a first paid period and 30 for a successful renewal period;
- no separately sold credit packs;
- credits are also earned only after a completed physical garment handoff;
- credits are redeemable only for physical garments supplied by another member;
- reciprocal one-garment-for-one-garment swaps can use zero credits;
- members may give away a garment for zero credits;
- no cash garment sale, seller payout, commission, shipping, auction, credit gifting, mixed cash/credit payment, or cash-out;
- credits do not expire and cannot create a negative balance;
- a separate optional digital AI Pro entitlement is not bundled with marketplace membership.

Fitly understands that Google Play Billing generally applies to digital subscriptions and sold virtual currency, while physical goods and peer-to-peer payments are excluded and earned or awarded points may be issued without Play Billing. Google also describes consumption-only apps and mixed digital/physical SKUs. Because the proposed membership combines marketplace access with units usable only for physical garment exchanges, we do not want to infer the classification.

Please confirm which of these contracts Google Play would accept for Canadian users:

1. A consumption-only Android app that shows server-derived membership status but contains no purchase flow, purchase link, price promotion, or external-purchase call to action, with the annual marketplace membership purchased on the web.
2. A Google Play subscription for marketplace membership that includes the first/renewal grant described above.
3. A paid membership separated from credits, with credits only independently awarded or earned from physical exchanges.
4. No paid membership in the app or pilot until a later reviewed submission.

For the accepted option, please also confirm the permitted behavior for purchase, restore, account management, cancellation, renewal disclosure, refund/revocation, and a provider-initiated stop-sale or wind-down. Fitly will not enable production billing until its product, legal, tax, privacy, and storefront approvals all describe the same contract.

Current implementation and required production invariants:

- Current development membership and credit balance mutations are server-authoritative.
- Existing development grants use immutable double-entry journal postings.
- C19 provider-event ownership, provider-period idempotency, and paid refund/revocation handling are not implemented.
- Before billing is enabled, the approved production design must prevent a refund or revocation from creating a negative balance or clawing earned credits back from an innocent counterparty.
- The proposed lapse behavior retains history, balance visibility, account deletion, and the ability to finish or dispute an already accepted physical handoff, but prevents a new marketplace transaction.
- Production billing is currently disabled; development activation is no-charge.

We can provide a review account, build, terms, disclosures, and lifecycle diagrams after the appropriate developer account and submission channel are available.
