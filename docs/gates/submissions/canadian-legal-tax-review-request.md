# Fitly Canadian legal and tax review request

**Draft status:** not approved and not sent

**Proposed launch scope:** invite-only Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley, British
Columbia pilot

## Requested opinion

Please provide a written Canadian and British Columbia legal/tax/accounting
opinion on the proposed Fitly marketplace contract below. Please state any facts
you relied on, qualifications, unresolved questions, required customer language,
required records, retention periods, operating registrations, filing/remittance
obligations, and changes required before a paid pilot.

## Proposed product facts

- Adults only.
- Proposed annual marketplace membership: CAD 59 plus applicable tax.
- Proposed server grants: 60 Fitly Credits for the first paid membership period
  and 30 for each successful renewal or late reactivation period.
- Credits are whole-number, closed-loop accounting units with no cash value.
- Credits cannot be purchased separately, gifted, transferred outside a
  completed garment transaction, cashed out, or used to create a negative
  balance.
- Credits do not expire.
- A listing owner may choose a whole-credit price from 1 to 60.
- A credit transaction transfers existing credits after a dual-confirmed
  physical garment handoff.
- A reciprocal one-garment-for-one-garment swap uses zero credits.
- A free peer give-away uses zero credits and makes no charitable status or tax
  receipt claim.
- The first release excludes cash sales, seller payouts, commissions, shipping,
  auctions, multi-item swaps, credit packs, credit gifting, mixed cash/credit
  payments, and cash-out.
- Fitly knows the parties, listing terms, accepted mode/credit amount, and final
  settlement state. It operates server-authoritative offers, reservations,
  private chat, dual confirmation, dispute freezes, and an immutable
  double-entry credit journal.
- Fitly does not collect a precise home or meetup address for marketplace
  discovery; it uses private coarse geography and a general pickup area.
- The digital AI Pro entitlement is separate from marketplace membership.
- Production billing is disabled and current development activation is free.

## Membership lifecycle proposed for review

- Membership lapse preserves balance, history, account deletion, and completion
  or dispute of an already accepted handoff, but blocks new listings, offers,
  acceptance, and transactions.
- Provider-reported billing grace preserves entitlement, but a renewal grant is
  not issued until payment recovery succeeds.
- Cancellation stops future renewal and takes effect at the paid-period end,
  unless the provider reports a refund or revocation.
- Restore replays provider-authoritative events and never duplicates a grant.
- Family sharing is disabled; each participant has an individual adult account
  and marketplace party.
- Account merges require verified common ownership, preserved journal/party
  provenance, a single surviving login, dual approval, and no duplicate
  first-period grant.
- Refund before any grant spend/reservation reverses an unused grant through an
  immutable journal. Refund, chargeback, or revocation after value moves cannot
  create a negative balance or claw earned value from an innocent member; it
  freezes new transactions for an approved operator resolution.
- A wind-down disables new grants/offers/acceptance first while preserving
  completion, cancellation, dispute, history, required records, notice, and
  provider stop-renewal actions.

## Legal questions requiring an explicit answer

1. Do the membership-linked, earned, or transferred Fitly Credit cohorts fall
   within B.C. prepaid purchase card/stored-value rules? What disclosures,
   replacement rights, restrictions, expiry prohibitions, fee prohibitions,
   dormant-balance, unclaimed-property, refund, and wind-down duties apply?
2. Does the proposed annual auto-renewing contract fall within the B.C.
   subscription-contract rules, and what pre-contract disclosure, contract-copy,
   30-to-60-day notice, cancellation, refund formula/timing, price-change, and
   proof-of-delivery controls are required?
3. What Terms of Use provisions are required for title, authority to transfer,
   condition mismatch, counterfeits, prohibited/unsafe/recalled/hygiene items,
   risk of loss, public meetup safety, no-shows, cancellations, returns,
   disputes, evidence, default outcomes, appeal, suspension, and termination?
4. What notice-and-takedown, repeat-infringer, counter-notice, report, block, and
   moderator processes are required for the planned launch geography?
5. What personal information may Fitly collect for seller due diligence,
   platform reporting, fraud, support, moderation, disputes, and tax records?
   Define role access, notice/consent, residency/transfer, legal holds, retention,
   scheduled destruction, backup re-deletion, and account-deletion boundaries.
6. Does the proposed activity make Fitly a reporting platform operator under
   Canadian Part XX rules? Identify relevant activities, excluded sellers,
   thresholds, due diligence, identity/TIN/date-of-birth requirements, reporting
   periods, seller statements, amendments, and record retention.
7. What reasonable, consistent Canadian-dollar fair-market-value method should
   apply to credit exchanges and direct swaps if non-fiat consideration must be
   reported? How should cancellations, reversals, give-aways, and platform-loss
   credits be treated?
8. Does Fitly or a participating member have GST/HST obligations for membership,
   garment-for-credit transactions, direct swaps, give-aways, marketplace
   services, or an administered barter-unit network? Should designation of a
   barter-exchange network be considered?
9. Does B.C. PST apply to the membership or any marketplace service, and is
   Fitly a marketplace facilitator or marketplace seller under the actual
   no-cash member settlement flow?
10. What legal entity, registrations, insurance, consumer-support contact,
    receipts, books and records, complaint handling, and geographic restrictions
    are required before an invite-only paid pilot?
